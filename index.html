Five days in the bay

Most bad wraps fail at the edges. That's why the first day goes on prep and taking parts off, before any film touches the car.

Day 1

Decon wash & clay

Iron remover, clay bar, then an isopropyl wipe on every panel so the adhesive grips paint, not wax.

~4 hr
Day 1

Disassembly

Handles, mirrors, badges, tail lights and trim come off so film wraps around edges instead of stopping at them.

~5 hr
Day 2–3

Lay & tuck

Film goes on one panel at a time, glassed out flat and tucked behind every edge. Knifeless tape for any cut near paint.

~20 hr
Day 4

Post-heat

Every stretched area and edge is heated to about 200°F with an IR thermometer, so the film stops trying to shrink back.

~3 hr
Day 5

Rest & inspect

The car sits overnight, then we reassemble, check every edge under a light and hand you the keys.

~2 hr

Keeping it looking new

Cast vinyl lasts five to seven years outdoors in Texas if you treat it like paint, not like a sticker.

Gloss

  • +Hand wash, two buckets
  • +Vinyl-safe spray sealant monthly
  • −No abrasive polish or compound

Satin & matte

  • +Matte-specific soap only
  • −No wax, it leaves shiny patches
  • −No brush car washes, ever

Everything

  • +Pressure washer at least 12" away
  • +Get bird droppings off the same day
  • −Don't aim the jet at edges or seams
